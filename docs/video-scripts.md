# Wink — Video Scripts (Colosseum Crypto World's Fair, Tempo track)

Two deliverables, both recorded from the live app + screen capture.
Read time targets: **Pitch 2:30–2:45** · **Demo 2:45–2:55**.
Voice: calm, confident, slightly playful. We are not pitching a hackathon
project — we are introducing a company.

**House rules for both videos**
- Every number on screen comes from a real transaction; the explorer link
  is shown for at least 2 seconds.
- Handles spoken as "@adaeze", never "adaeze dot something".
- Closing line is always the same: *"Anyone, anywhere, paid with a wink."*
- No jargon dump: one mention of Tempo, one of pathUSD, one of stablecoins.
  Everything else is human language.

## VIDEO 1 — PITCH (2:30–2:45)

### COLD OPEN — 0:00–0:18
**Screen:** black. A single chat bubble types itself out:
`wink @adaeze $5` — then a phone notification: *"You've been winked 😉 $5.00"*.

**Narration:**
> "The last time someone paid you, what did they type? A twenty-six character
> address? An account number and a sort code?
> Payments got faster. But they never got *human*.
> Wink fixes that — with one primitive: **pay a name.**"

### THE PROBLEM — 0:18–0:40
**Screen:** montage — a failed bank transfer screenshot, a copied wallet
address with two characters highlighted wrong, a creator holding a paper QR.

**Narration:**
> "Today, getting paid means friction everywhere it hurts most.
> Creators paste wallet addresses and pray nobody fat-fingers a character.
> Barbers and stylists lose five minutes per client to transfer apps.
> Remote teams wire money across borders and lose four percent to fees.
> And the people doing all this work? They don't think in addresses.
> They think in names."

### THE PRODUCT — 0:40–1:15
**Screen:** live app. Type `@adaeze` into the wink box. $5. Message
*"For the hair cut 🙌"*. Confirm. The ledger row lands. Then the wedding
spray wall with winks raining. Then a printed wink-code QR on a shop counter.

**Narration:**
> "Wink is the name layer for payments.
> Claim a handle — `@adaeze`. Now anyone can pay you with three words:
> **wink, adaeze, five dollars.**
> Tips at a wedding rain on a live spray wall. A barber prints one QR —
> a wink code — and every client pays with an invoice attached. A founder
> pastes a payroll list and pays five people in one run.
> One name. Every kind of money-in."

### THE RAILS — 1:15–1:45
**Screen:** diagram card: "ANY ASSET, ANY CHAIN → pathUSD on Tempo".
Then the fee receipt: recipient $10.00, sender −$10.008, zero gas.

**Narration:**
> "We build on Tempo, the chain designed for payments — stablecoin-native,
> sub-second settlement, and fees in the stablecoin itself. No gas tokens,
> no surprises. We measured it: a ten-dollar wink arrives as ten dollars.
> Recipients always get the full amount. Our platform fee is zero percent.
> And whatever a payer starts with — any asset, any chain — it settles as
> pathUSD on Tempo. Always. One balance, one currency, no babysitting."

### THE BUSINESS — 1:45–2:10
**Screen:** GTM one-pager scrolling: creators → owambes → barbershops →
remote payroll. Then the handle marketplace mock: short handles as premium.

**Narration:**
> "The rails are free. The names are the business.
> Wink grows the way payments always grow — beachheads. Nigerian creators
> first, then event hosts, then the barbershop on every corner, then remote
> payroll. Every wink sent is an onboarding: the recipient sees their name
> move money, and claims their own handle.
> When a hundred thousand names exist, the short ones become valuable —
> that's where we monetize. Premium handles."

### CLOSE — 2:10–2:40
**Screen:** founder on camera (or avatar), then the logo + the one-liner.

**Narration:**
> "Wink is built by a team that has felt every one of these frictions —
> in Lagos, where the world already tips, sprays and hustles in chat.
> We built it on Tempo because payments deserve a chain built for payments.
> We are Wink.
> **Anyone, anywhere, paid with a wink.**"

**End card:** wink.cash · built on Tempo · Colosseum World's Fair 2026

## VIDEO 2 — DEMO (2:45–2:55)

> Format: screen capture, no face cam needed. Cursor moves slowly. Every
> on-chain moment is followed by the explorer tab. The founder's voice is
> the only voice. Rehearse ≥3 full takes; the bridge moment ≥10.

### INTRO — 0:00–0:12
**Screen:** landing page, live totals ticking.

**Narration:**
> "This is Wink. One primitive — pay a @username — across every way money
> comes in. Everything you're about to see settles on Tempo, and every
> transaction gets verified on the explorer, live."

### JOURNEY 1 — THE SPRAY WALL — 0:12–0:55
**Screen:** `/wall/adaeze-arinzaay-s-wedding-ggdny5` on the left (TV mode),
guest phone flow on the right.

**Narration:**
> "Journey one — events. Adaeze and Arinzaay are getting married. Guests
> scan one QR, and instead of envelopes, they wink.
> Watch the wall."
*(guest sends $10 with message "Congrats to the happy couple 🥂")*
> "The wink lands on the wall in real time — and on-chain."
*(open explorer tab, show the TransferWithMemo event, memo decoded)*
> "That memo is Wink's fingerprint: `wk_` plus the transfer id. The chain
> is the truth; the ledger is the product."

### JOURNEY 2 — THE MERCHANT — 0:55–1:35
**Screen:** dashboard → wink codes → print-style QR for INV-042, then the
payer's phone.

**Narration:**
> "Journey two — a real shop. Adaeze runs a barbering studio. She creates
> an invoice wink code: amount fixed at two-fifty, memo INV-042.
> The client scans, sees exactly what they're paying for, and confirms."
*(payment confirms; dashboard shows the sale row with INV-042)*
> "No terminal. No cash reconciliation. Every sale carries its invoice
> number on-chain — her books balance themselves."

### JOURNEY 3 — GETTING PAID — 1:35–2:15
**Screen:** `/request` → Arinzaay requests $15 → Adaeze pays. Then the
payroll screen with a pasted list.

**Narration:**
> "Journey three — money out. Arinzaay designed the wedding website. He
> sends a pay request; Adaeze approves it in one tap."
*(confirm; explorer receipt shown)*
> "Now scale it. Arinzaay runs a studio with contractors. He pastes a
> list — names and amounts — Wink validates every handle, and pays
> everyone in one run."
*(payroll executes: $12 → @arinzaay, $8 → @ngozi; ledger shows kind=wage)*
> "Paste a list. Pay everyone. Every row lands on Tempo as a wage."

### THE FOURTH DOOR + THE CROSSING — 2:15–2:45
**Screen:** `/agents` — the 402 challenge → agent pays → 200 + receipt.
Then the bridge quote screen: Base USDC → Tempo pathUSD.

**Narration:**
> "Two more things. First — machines. An AI agent wants Wink's analytics.
> It gets an HTTP 402, pays a quarter in pathUSD, and gets its receipt.
> That's the Tempo Machine Payments Protocol — and Wink speaks it.
> Second — the crossing. A payer holds USDC on Base. Wink quotes the route,
> takes one signature, and delivers pathUSD to the recipient's handle on
> Tempo. Any asset, any chain in — Tempo out. Always."

### OUTRO — 2:45–2:55
**Screen:** landing page proof strip, then end card.

**Narration:**
> "Tips, invoices, payroll, machines — one name layer for all of it.
> We are Wink. Anyone, anywhere, paid with a wink."

**End card:** wink.cash · built on Tempo
