😉 wink.cash
The name layer for payments · built on Tempo · Colosseum Crypto World's Fair

Anyone, anywhere, paid with a wink.

Wink replaces wallet addresses with @handles and settles everything as stablecoins (pathUSD) on Tempo — ~500ms finality, zero gas, 0% platform fee. One primitive (pay a @username) powers four money paths: 🎉 events🏪 shops💸 payroll🤖 agents

Why Nigeria first

The pain is loudest here

Currency volatility pushes everyday people toward dollar assets; receiving money — tips, sales, wages from abroad — is still friction-heavy. Stablecoins on a fast, fee-light chain answer a daily problem, not a hypothetical one.

The culture is the distribution

Nigeria already runs on social money: owambes spray cash on celebrants, creators get tipped by fans, barbershops live by the counter. Wink doesn't change the culture — it puts the culture on rails.

Three beachheads, one loop

1 · Owambes & eventsSpray wall QR at weddings/parties. Guests scan, winks rain live. Every guest experiences paying by @handle — the send is the onboarding.
2 · Counter commerceBarbershops, salons, food vendors print a pay code. Invoices carry references; the sale reconciles itself. Zero hardware, zero fees.
3 · Remote payWorkers request, payers approve with one tap, wages land in a @handle — no bank details, no borders, no waiting days.

Growth loop: guest scans wall → pays in seconds with zero setup → sees their own name missing → claims a handle → hosts their own wall / prints their own code. Events recruit merchants; merchants recruit workers; workers become creators.

Traction model & monetization

Acquisition: event hosts (free, viral by design) → QR-printed merchants → creator/worker referrals. Retention: unified money-in dashboard, private support history, invoice reconciliation. Revenue: the rails are free — premium short handles are the business (@chop, @fade, @aso), plus hosted insights for event hosts and merchants later. No platform fee on transfers, ever, in v1.
